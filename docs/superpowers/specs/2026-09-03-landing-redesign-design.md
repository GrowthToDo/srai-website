# SimpleRosterAI landing redesign — Design Spec

Date: 2026-09-03
Status: approved 2026-09-03

`$SRC` = the sibling US repo path, kept outside this repository. `$VID` = the
sibling US site's Remotion launch-video project, also outside this repository.

## 1. Goal

Rebuild the home page as a Pain–Person–Promise landing page on a new visual
system that no longer resembles the sibling US site, restyle the inner pages to
match, replace the logo, fix the founder photo, correct the hours claim to the
Indian reality, and produce an Indian-context launch video for the hero.

## 2. Decisions (from the 2026-09-03 brainstorm)

- Hours: the engine enforces "the weekly hours cap you set". No page claims a
  48-hour week as a legal or universal rule. The demo defaults its cap to 48
  and labels it "the ward's hours cap (48h in this demo)".
- Video: fork `$VID` into `D:\Pradeep\Personal\Projects\srai-launch-video`
  (outside this repo), retheme, rewrite scenes, render landscape and vertical.
- Look: deep navy ground, saffron accent, teal for "passed", Manrope headings,
  Inter body. Reads clearly different from the sibling site (forest green,
  ivory, Fraunces).
- Scope: home page rebuilt; inner pages restyled through shared tokens and
  components, with copy edits limited to the hours claim and the Indian-context
  audit.
- Scroll lock: one pinned section ("How it works") on desktop only; plain
  stacked list on mobile and under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 3. Design tokens

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--aw-color-bg-page` | `#F7F8FA` (cool off-white) | light sections |
| `--aw-color-bg-page-dark` | `#0B1F3A` (deep navy) | hero, dark bands, footer |
| `--aw-color-primary` | `#F5A623` (saffron) | primary button, highlighted phrase, marks |
| `--aw-color-secondary` | `#D98F0F` | primary hover |
| `--aw-rgb-ink` | `11 31 58` | text on light |
| `--aw-rgb-ivory` | `247 248 250` | text on dark |
| `--aw-rgb-sage` (renamed use: "teal") | `42 167 155` | passed states, secondary accents on dark |
| `--aw-font-heading` | `'Manrope Variable'` | h1–h3 |
| `--aw-font-sans` | `'Inter Variable'` | body |

Tailwind class names (`primary`, `ink`, `ivory`, `sage`) are kept so existing
components restyle without edits; only `CustomStyles.astro` values change.
`@fontsource-variable/manrope` replaces `@fontsource-variable/fraunces`.
Buttons: primary is saffron with navy text; secondary on dark is an ivory
outline; secondary on light is a navy outline.

## 4. Logo

Wordmark "SimpleRosterAI" in Manrope 700 with a mark: three arcs (morning,
evening, night) of increasing length arranged as thirds of a ring, saffron,
with a small navy dot at the centre. The mark reads as a duty wheel. Delivered
as inline SVG in `Logo.astro` (header and footer), `src/assets/favicons/favicon.svg`,
`public/icon.svg`, PNG favicons regenerated by `scripts/build-icons.py`, and the
OG image regenerated by `scripts/build-social.py` on navy with the mark.

## 5. Home page structure and copy budget

Order, each with its purpose and a hard word budget for body text:

1. **Header** (sticky): logo, links Home / How it works / Demo / Pricing /
   Resources, saffron "Book a demo".
2. **Hero** (navy): eyebrow "FOR CNOs AND NURSING SUPERINTENDENTS"; h1 "Your
   in-charges spend a third of their week on the duty roster. <span
   saffron>Get it back.</span>"; one-sentence promise "The engine builds every
   ward's roster against your rules, nurses see it on their phones, your
   nursing office approves."; CTAs "Book a demo" / "Try the interactive demo";
   the launch video in a rounded navy card (autoplay, muted, loop,
   `preload="metadata"`, poster PNG, paused under reduced motion). Backdrop:
   the sibling site's nurse-station photo is NOT used; the hero uses a subtle
   radial saffron glow and grain only, keeping the look distinct. 40 words max.
3. **Proof strip** (light): three tiles: "77 duty seats a week, filled in one
   click", "21 automated checks on every draft", "Nurses see duties on their
   phones, nothing to install". No body text.
4. **Pain** (light): h2 "Good wards run on a roster nobody trusts"; five
   numbered callouts, 18 words each max: 01 Version drift (board, Excel,
   WhatsApp photo disagree by Wednesday); 02 The hours recount (every change
   rechecked by hand, overtime found after payroll); 03 Night rotation fights
   (the same nurses on nights, the same complaints); 04 Leave clashes (CL
   approved on paper, duty still on the grid); 05 The 2 AM absence (phone
   tree instead of a shortlist).
5. **How it works** (navy, pinned on desktop): left sticky panel with four
   steps that activate as the right column scrolls through four cards: 01
   Staff list in (the Excel you already keep); 02 Three drafts out (balanced,
   fairness, cost, checked against your rules); 03 Nurses on their phones
   (duties, leave, swaps, absences); 04 Nursing office approves (nothing
   publishes without sign-off). Each card 25 words max, one illustrative
   mini-screen per card reusing the existing "four screens" markup.
6. **Comparison** (light): h2 "Roster in Excel vs roster with SimpleRosterAI";
   six rows: building the month, a leave approval, an absence at 2 AM,
   night and weekend fairness, hours and overtime, audit trail. Left column
   red-tinted, right column teal check.
7. **Live demo teaser** (light): headline "Try it on a sample ward", the
   existing `SchedulerDemoSection`-style embed of the interactive demo grid
   with a "Try the interactive demo" CTA.
8. **Pricing** (navy band): ₹400 per nurse per month, ₹4,000 per year, GST
   extra, only nurses billed; link to /pricing.
9. **Fit** (light): h2 "You are a fit if"; five bullets: 300+ beds or a group;
   rosters built in Excel or an HRMS module that does not enforce rules;
   in-charges losing days to rostering; overtime and contract cover you cannot
   explain; NABH audits that need hours and rest reports. One line: "Not yet a
   fit: hospitals under 100 beds, or wards already on a rules-enforcing roster
   system."
10. **Team** (light): Gautham block, photo eager-loaded, re-encoded as RGB
    PNG 192×192, `width`/`height` set, no `loading="lazy"`.
11. **FAQ**: six existing questions, hours answer updated.
12. **Final CTA** (navy): "See it on one of your own wards." with both CTAs.

Body text across the page stays under 450 words. Every section has one
heading and at most one paragraph.

## 6. Components

New or changed, each with one responsibility:

- `src/components/Logo.astro` — new mark and wordmark (props: `variant:
  'light' | 'dark'`).
- `src/components/widgets/HeroVideo.astro` — video card with poster and
  reduced-motion handling; props `src`, `poster`, `label`.
- `src/components/widgets/ProofStrip.astro` — three tiles from a `tiles` prop.
- `src/components/widgets/PainList.astro` — numbered callouts from an `items`
  prop.
- `src/components/widgets/PinnedSteps.astro` — sticky left rail + scrolling
  cards; IntersectionObserver sets the active step; no pinning below `lg` or
  under reduced motion. Props `steps` (title, body, screen slot index).
- `src/components/widgets/Comparison.astro` — two-column table from `rows`.
- `src/components/widgets/FitList.astro` — bullets plus "not yet a fit" line.
- `src/components/widgets/DemoTeaser.astro` — wraps the existing
  `DemoScheduler` in a section with heading and CTA (replaces the old
  `SchedulerDemoSection` idea; the old SSAI `SchedulerDemo.svelte` is not
  copied).
- `src/pages/index.astro` — composes the above; no inline section markup
  beyond the Gautham block.
- `src/components/CustomStyles.astro`, `tailwind.config.js`,
  `src/assets/styles/tailwind.css` — token values and button styles.
- `DarkHero.astro`, `CallToAction.astro`, `Header.astro`, `Footer.astro` —
  restyled by tokens; Header gains a subtle navy bottom border and the
  saffron button; Footer moves to navy with the new logo.
